How is AI really being put to work?

We are interviewing people who build and deploy AI systems for other organisations: founders, engineers, forward-deployed engineers and consultants at AI startups and agencies. You see how adoption actually happens; we want to learn from that.

  • An AI interviewer asks about one client firm you worked with, how each level of that firm was affected, and a similar firm that did not go ahead.
  • It takes about 30 minutes and is done by voice, so you need a microphone and a quiet place.
  • Please do not name clients, prospective clients or individuals. Answers are used for research only and reported in aggregate.
  • You can stop at any time. Say "stop" or press the end-call button to finish.

This is a pilot of the interview. Conversations are recorded and transcribed by ElevenLabs on behalf of the research team. Questions: Adrien Joly, LSE.
